Is the Model Any Good?

PR-AUC 0.699 against a 0.204 no-skill baseline. ROC-AUC 0.861. Brier 0.103.

Logistic regression baseline: PR-AUC 0.464.

A few real quirks show up. Age is the strongest single driver. NumOfProducts is nonlinear: 2 products is safest at 7.6% churn, 3 products churns at 82.7%, and 4 products at 100%. Germany churns at roughly twice the rate of France and Spain, and about a third of customers hold a zero balance.

Value proxy: value_saved = 120 * num_products + 0.02 * balance. The $120 per product is an annual relationship value order of magnitude for retail banking (fees, interchange, cross-sell). The 2% is a net interest margin on deposits. A pure balance proxy would degenerate on the roughly one third of customers who hold a zero balance, valuing them at zero, so the per-product term keeps multi-product customers visible to the engine.

This targets customers by churn probability, not persuadability. True incrementality would need uplift modeling and treatment data this dataset does not have.
